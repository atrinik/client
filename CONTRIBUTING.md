# Independent implementation

Direct human-written code contributions are welcome. The project currently
develops software primarily through Codex-driven agentic workflows, but changes
written by people or agents follow the same accountable review, provenance,
licensing, testing, and repository-validation requirements.

The current client foundation is newly authored MIT work from public
specifications, observable behavior, interoperability facts, synthetic
fixtures, independently licensed data, and permissive dependencies; no
historical grant was used for it. Later exact reuse of eligible historical
Classic source is permitted only under the process below. Otherwise,
independent implementation remains the default, and unadmitted GPL material
must not be consulted as an implementation template, copied, transliterated,
mechanically translated, or structurally preserved. Mixed content and assets
remain external under their own terms and never become MIT by association.

Every module, reused file, behavior-only migration, generated input, fixture,
and asset updates `PROVENANCE.md` and the machine manifests. Historical reuse
follows the canonical
[`atrinik/atrinik` grantor registry](https://github.com/atrinik/atrinik/blob/main/docs/PROVENANCE.md)
and requires each selected, independently separable contribution to fit one
applicable row's “original past Atrinik contributions solely authored” scope.
Historical rows cannot be combined to cover a jointly authored contribution,
agent-generated output, or inseparable mixed work; later or agent-generated
material needs its own contemporaneous compatible rights. Record complete,
non-shallow, rename-aware history; identity and authorship evidence; embedded
material review; exact source and destination revisions and paths;
transformation, reviewer, license, and notice decisions; and the exact registry
revision. Tests, fixtures, generated output, assets, and dependency code receive
no presumption of coverage. This source-reuse route does not authorize GPL/AGPL
dependencies or bundles. The checked-in Classic distribution remains
GPL-2.0-or-later; MIT permission applies only to the exact selected material
recorded for the destination. Uncertainty fails closed.

Pull requests confirm: no unrecorded GPL input; exact authorship/design inputs;
generated input/tool/drift evidence; fixture and asset licenses/digests/notices;
dependency/native policy; bounded failure/privacy behavior; and
`tools/validate.sh`.
